## Product policy

1. Discover and rank activities independently of monetization. Affiliate eligibility, payout and sponsorship must never enter source priority, coverage targets or fit scores.
2. Keep the official source URL unchanged and visible. A commercial booking link is a separately labeled option for the exact same activity, date, venue and ticket type.
3. Compare total price, mandatory fees, cancellation terms and availability. Do not steer visitors to a worse booking option just to earn a commission.
4. Preserve free and non-affiliate activities with equal treatment. The weekly source maintainer continues to prioritize real coverage gaps, not merchant catalogs.
5. Require human verification of product matching and approved account terms before activation. Never manufacture affiliate tracking parameters or assume all links on a platform are commissionable.
6. Keep link metadata explicit if exposed to agents: original source URL, merchant booking URL, whether it is an affiliate link, disclosure text and the last mapping check. Do not silently replace canonical URLs in the public skill or JSON. Confirm that agent-generated sharing is a permitted channel before distributing those links through the skill.

An illustrative calculation, not a revenue forecast: ten qualifying completed $60 bookings at 8% would yield $48 before tax or other adjustments. Actual earnings depend on relevant traffic, conversions, attribution, merchant terms, cancellations and completion. Useful local coverage is the priority; affiliate revenue may simply offset some costs.

## Low-effort pilot, after core work

Start with one approved program and three to five genuinely useful activity pages already worth recommending. Use a separate booking button with plain-language commission disclosure nearby; preserve the official organizer link. Use the affiliate platform's reporting before building our own tracking infrastructure. Review whether visitors benefit and whether revenue justifies maintenance. No database or server-side checkout is needed for a link-based pilot.

For web links, use `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` where appropriate. Plain-language disclosure should explain the payment relationship, for example: “We may earn a commission if you book through this link.” The label “affiliate link” alone may not communicate that clearly. Keep disclosure adjacent to the relevant booking option, not only on a remote policy page. See the [FTC affiliate disclosure guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking#affiliate).

## Hosting decision before activation

[GitHub Pages limits](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/github-pages-limits) prohibit using Pages as free hosting for an online business/e-commerce site or a site primarily directed at facilitating commercial transactions. The documentation does not establish an explicit affiliate-link exception for this project. Before monetization, confirm the intended editorial use with GitHub or move the static site to an appropriate host. Do not assume current hosting is approved for a monetized product, and do not migrate hosting or grant another provider repository access without authorization.

Keep this below coverage quality, reliable collection and the versioned agent skill in the backlog. Recheck program availability, rates and hosting terms when the pilot is actually undertaken.
