# Day-trip entry prompt

> Use $kc-activity-finder to help me plan a day trip from **[starting city or neighborhood]**, within **[X hours] of driving each way**. First compare destinations with compelling things to do, rather than listing nearby events. Consider permanent attractions, outdoor recreation, current exhibits and date-specific activities. Tell me what makes each destination worth the drive, its strongest one or two activities, other nearby options, estimated outbound/return driving, costs and major tradeoffs. Include adults-only, kids or family options according to **[who is coming / ages if relevant]** and **[interests]**. The date is **[date, date range, or flexible]**. Search beyond the usual KC/Lawrence/Columbia/Bentonville sources when the drive limit makes other places relevant. Keep unknown hours, eligibility, prices and availability explicit. After I pick a destination, build a relaxed itinerary with meals, local transfers, parking/rest buffers, booking actions, an appropriate weather backup and a return by **[time]**. Verify official hours and activity dates, cite sources, and distinguish estimates from confirmed facts.

A shorter natural request should trigger the same workflow:

> I want a day trip within two hours of Kansas City. What places have something really worth going for? We can pick a destination first and plan the day after.

For an adults-only variation:

> Plan an adults-only day trip from [origin], within [X] hours each way, around [art / food / music / hiking / other interests]. Compare destinations first; then help us plan the best fit.

For a family variation:

> Help us pick a day trip from [origin], within [X] hours each way, for [adults and children's ages]. Prioritize [interests] and a realistic pace, including [nap / stroller / accessibility / return-time needs].

Use only the information supplied. Missing date is allowed for destination exploration; missing origin or driving limit needs clarification. Interpret an unlabeled limit as one way and state that assumption. Ask rather than assuming when the wording could mean a total driving budget. Users do not need to fill every bracket or provide a home address.
